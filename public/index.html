Mindmate

A private space to check in with how you're feeling.

What this is. Somewhere to notice how you're doing, write things down, and see it over time.

What it isn't. It will never diagnose you or tell you what's wrong. It isn't a counsellor and it isn't an emergency service. When something is serious, it points you to a person.

You can export everything or delete all of it at any time, from the Data tab.
