# Support page content — must be verified before use

The Support screen currently shows placeholders. **Do not ship or demo this app to real
students until these are filled in with details checked against the official source on the
day you check them.** A wrong crisis number is worse than no number at all, because someone
may dial it at the moment they most need it to work.

## What has to be filled in

### 1. Crisis lines (`#view-support`, the amber card)

Verify each of these directly on the organisation's own website — not from a search-result
snippet, a screenshot, or an AI answer, including this file:

- **SADAG** (South African Depression and Anxiety Group) — <https://www.sadag.org>
  They run several lines for different purposes (suicide crisis, general counselling,
  substance abuse). Record which line is which, the hours each one is open, and whether
  there is a WhatsApp or SMS option, since data is cheaper than a call for most students.
- **Emergency services** — the number to call when someone is in immediate physical danger.
- Any national line your institution officially points students to.

For each entry record: name, number, hours, cost (toll-free or not), and language options.

### 2. Campus counselling (`#view-support`, second card)

This is the part generic apps get wrong and the part that makes Mindmate specific to SA
students. For your institution:

- Name of the student counselling / wellness service
- Building and room, and a landmark to find it
- Opening hours, including whether it closes during vacations
- How to book: walk-in, phone, email, online form
- Whether it is free (it usually is, and students often do not know this)
- After-hours arrangements

If your group is targeting more than one campus, this becomes a small lookup rather than
fixed text, and it is the one screen that legitimately needs a connection.

## Rules for writing the copy

Taken from the research in the project brief:

1. **Never diagnose.** No wording that tells a user what condition they have or might have.
2. **No urgency theatre.** Calm, plain sentences. Someone reading this may already be
   overwhelmed.
3. **Say what will happen.** "You will speak to a counsellor" beats "get help now".
4. **Name the cost.** Students avoid help they think they cannot afford.
5. **Keep it text.** No images on this screen — it must load on a bad connection.

## When the numbers are in

Remove the `placeholder-note` paragraphs from `index.html`, add the real content, and add
the date you verified it plus who verified it. Re-check every term, and put a reminder in
the group's calendar — these details change.
